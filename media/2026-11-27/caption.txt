The Dallas mall that's also an art museum 🇺🇸

In 1965 Raymond Nasher opened NorthPark Center on a former cotton field. Today Andy Warhol silkscreens line one corridor and Jonathan Borofsky's Five Hammering Men stand in the South Court. Over 27 million people visit every year.
