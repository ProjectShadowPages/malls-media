The Singapore airport mall with the world's tallest indoor waterfall 🇸🇬

At Jewel Changi Airport the HSBC Rain Vortex drops 40 metres through a hole in the glass roof. Around it rise a five-storey forest and about 300 shops and restaurants. After dark the water becomes a light show.

Would you get to the airport early just for this? 👇
