This Edmonton mall has the world's largest indoor wave pool 🇨🇦

West Edmonton Mall opened in 1981 and was the biggest mall on Earth until 2004. Inside: a waterpark with two-metre waves, a lake where submarines once gave tours, and a replica of Columbus's Santa María.

Would you go for the shops or the waves? 👇
