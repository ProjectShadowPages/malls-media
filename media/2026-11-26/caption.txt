The Doha mall with a 150-metre Venice canal inside 🇶🇦

Villaggio Mall opened in Qatar's Aspire Zone in 2006. Gondolas glide along an indoor canal under a painted sky to Via Domo, home of Gucci and Cartier. There's even an Olympic-size ice rink.

Would you take the gondola or just walk? 👇
