From the Belly of Paris to an underground mall 🇫🇷

For 800 years this was the main food market of Paris – Émile Zola called it “the Belly of Paris”. Then the halls were torn down and left a huge hole in the city. In 1979 a shopping centre opened in its place, and since 2016 it hides under a 2.5-hectare glass canopy. 🏛️

Market or mall – which would you rather have seen? 👇
