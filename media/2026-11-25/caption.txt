The Metro Manila mall with the world's largest IKEA store 🇵🇭

SM Mall of Asia sits on reclaimed land by Manila Bay in Pasay. Nearly one million people came on opening day in 2006. Today it has 663 shops, an Olympic-size ice rink and an IKEA of 65,000 m².
